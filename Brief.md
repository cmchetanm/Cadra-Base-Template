# The scenario
You are joining Larkspur Health, a seed-stage startup that builds voice and chat agents that talk to patients on behalf of clinics. Larkspur's first customer is Saguaro Family Medicine.

## Context
Saguaro runs fourteen primary care clinics: eight in Arizona (Phoenix and Tucson), four in Nevada (Las Vegas) and two in New Mexico (Albuquerque). It has about 75,000 active patients, and a little over 40% of them are on Medicare or a Medicare Advantage plan.

About 30% of Saguaro's patients list Spanish as their preferred language. Some clinics have bilingual front desk staff. Most do not.

Maya Ortiz, Larkspur's co-founder, in her own words:

"Saguaro's phone lines are drowning. They take around 2,800 calls a day and Monday morning hold times are over twenty minutes. After 5pm everything goes to an answering service that pages the on-call doctor for things a nurse could handle, and misses things it shouldn't. We want one agent that answers the phone, the text line and the chat on the patient portal. It should book and move appointments, take refill requests, answer the 'is my MRI approved yet' and 'do you take my plan' questions, and make the follow-up calls Saguaro owes patients within two business days of a hospital discharge. I'd love it to tell patients whether they need to come in. Saguaro's privacy officer asked me exactly one question in our last meeting: where does the audio go? Their front desk lives in Epic. We go live at one clinic in eight weeks."

2. What comes in
Five recent contacts, with names and details changed:

A. Phone, Las Vegas clinic line, Monday 7:52am. "I need my lisinopril refilled. And my insurance changed on January 1st, I'm on a Humana plan now. Do I need to do anything?"
B. Portal chat, Thursday 11:40pm. "my dad was discharged from Desert Valley Medical Center yesterday for his heart failure. tonight he's more short of breath than this morning and his ankles look bigger. should I be worried"
C. Phone, Tucson clinic line, Tuesday 2:15pm. "Hola, quiero saber si ya aprobaron la resonancia de mi esposo. El doctor la pidió hace dos semanas."
D. Text, from a number not on file. "this is Dana Whitfield DOB 3/14/1961 can you text me my A1C results"
E. Phone, from a 612 area code, February. "I'm down here for the winter, staying in Mesa, 85206. Can somebody see me this week for a cough that won't go away? I've never been to you before."
Each of these needs something different. Working out what, and what the agent should and should not do on its own, is part of the job.
